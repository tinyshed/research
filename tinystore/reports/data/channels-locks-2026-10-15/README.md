reviewed product/harness revisions. All input fixtures are deterministic and
generated by the committed source. No downloaded/private corpus is included.

There are 279 sequential successful measured/check processes. The generator
asserts counts, case group sizes, success flags, non-overlapping UTC intervals,
and channel delivery/loss arithmetic. It never drops a slow pass.
