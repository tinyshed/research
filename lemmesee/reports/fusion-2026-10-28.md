# Meaning and results before the bottleneck, 28 October 2026

Round 1j tests early fusion, residual learning, and the representation of
round 1h's frozen title student. **No configuration establishes a substantial,
consistent improvement over the numeric look. No new model is kept.**
The harness and a proposed next architecture are in [round-1j/](../round-1j/).

| Question | Answer | What follows |
|---|---|---|
| Does feeding per-slot word vectors and lexical properties directly to the look help? | Sonnet keeps 3.09 of five against 2.94; Gemini 3.19 against 3.10; Opus 2.83 against 2.96. The paired intervals include zero. | Early fusion of these frozen features is not an established gain. |
| Do explicit semantic/result products help a linear learner? | Sonnet 2.30 and Gemini 2.57; both clearly below the baseline. | Replacing the nonlinear numeric model loses useful behavior. |
| Does preserving that model and learning a residual correction fix it? | Early residual: Sonnet 2.69, Gemini 2.93; numeric and late-scalar correction controls stay near the baseline. | A residual alone does not resolve the representation problem. |
| Does the title student's single probability discard useful learned state? | Its 600 tree contributions yield Sonnet 3.05, Gemini 3.15, Opus 3.05; their sum alone yields 3.06, 3.30, 2.88. Neither retained state nor a 64-dimensional projection clearly improves the five. | Retaining a frozen representation is not evidence for or against jointly learning a contextual encoder. |
| Is there another engineering finding? | An old float32 threshold format changed an early-fusion prediction by 0.007 in a packing check. LMJ2 retains double thresholds; all readers now agree within 1e-5. | Dense features need a branch-preserving packed format. |

## Inputs and protocol

The existing 93 public databases, 90 schema families and 2,632 candidates
of rounds 1d–1f. No database was queried, no teacher was asked, and no paid
service was used. The targets are the existing Jev/DeepSeek/GLM mean keep.
Sonnet and Gemini count 50 databases; Opus counts 26. AUC excludes databases
on which a judge gives only one class: Gemini has 48.

Five family folds, three draws, seed 20261101. Every prediction is out of
family. Means below average the three selections within a database, then
databases. Paired confidence intervals resample families, 5,000 times.
Normalisation reads training rows only. Residual training additionally
uses three inner family folds for its baseline margins. There are no
tuned penalties or parameter choices made from the counting labels.

These are development comparisons on already inspected data, not a fresh
held-out confirmation. The original scores are staged in float32 arrays;
use this round's matched baseline, rather than subtracting an older report.
Counting saved per-panel labels cannot establish the usefulness of a newly
assembled five as a set, nor human preference.

## What the representations actually test

The early input is 127 existing numeric features, 104 per-slot semantic
features and 14 result-representation features. The word vectors are
round 1h's frozen 16-dimensional vectors, which retain 24% of the original
vocabulary variance; they are not a contextual language encoder.

The frozen student's scalar, full tree contributions and their projection
all use exactly the same compiled encoder. Its contribution sum reconstructs
the reader's score exactly. This controls the compression comparison,
including the compiled encoder's existing threshold quantisation.

The new tree format uses 16-bit feature indices and float64 thresholds.
The largest look tree weights are 109,608 bytes; a linear joint model is
43,332 bytes. These sizes exclude dictionaries and the frozen title
student. No combined production memory or inference-time claim is made.

## Handoff

Keep the numeric look as the control. Do not carry an early-fusion,
residual or frozen-representation model from this round into the product.
The next architectural hypothesis is a
[shared contextual encoder](../round-1j/shared-context.md): one typed schema
graph, contextual column representations, and jointly trained key, role
and question/result heads. A discriminating comparison must separate
adding context from sharing parameters. This architecture is proposed,
not tested by the frozen-encoder experiments above.
